## 0145 — the ชั้นปี model

Two independent defects, both reproduced from the authority before anything was
written. Full write-up: `docs/mistakes/postgres-schema.md`.

1. **`person_mirror_down` pushed `people.year` down; nothing carried it up.** So
   `update_my_identity`'s own last statement (an unrelated `update people`)
   reverted the edit the card had just made. Proved in a rollback transaction:
   `tm=5 → tm=3 → tm=5`.
2. **ระบบบ้าน derived ชั้นปี, ทีม SAMO stored it.** `house/fields.js` had
   predicted this in a comment since 0131 and it prevented nothing: 9 of 400
   members were a year behind, 13 more had a ชั้นปี and no รหัส, 3 had a รหัส and
   no ชั้นปี.

The fix moved the rule to `src/js/study-year.js` (a rule two systems need does
not belong inside one of them) and moved the INGREDIENTS to the placements:
`team_members` gained `cohort_year` + `year_offset`, mirrored down from
`public.people`, with `people_fill_cohort` giving the registry 0128's
re-derivation rule.

**Three ordering/safety details that were each nearly wrong** — see the mistakes
entry: the backfill must run AFTER the new trigger body (or it blanks 109
postings for the still-served bundle); the backfill CONVERTS a stored ชั้นปี into
a ปีที่เข้า rather than discarding it; and `person_mirror_down` must save/restore
`app.team_sync` rather than blank it, or a member with two ตำแหน่ง cannot save.

**The owner's question** — "should changing รหัสนักศึกษา change ชั้นปี, i think
it shouldn't" — was answered yes, with the reasoning that their instinct is
already satisfied by `year_offset` being a DIFFERENCE. Written into
`study-year.js`'s header and pinned by two tests.

## 0146 — the crest refcount

Carried over from the 2026-08-09 handoff. `photo_reference_count()` answered 0
for every house crest because it counted five `photo_url` columns and the crest
is `icon_url`. The guard test written for exactly this hazard scanned for
`photo_url` and reported green. Write-up: `docs/mistakes/integrations.md`.
